How to sell group tickets and tables

One person often buys for the whole group. DoorPass lets a buyer order several tickets or a whole table, send each seat to a friend, and get one receipt, while every guest still gets their own QR code.

Request access See the live demo

The steps

  • Choose single tickets or tables. In the admin, open the event and go to Ticket types. Each type is either a Single ticket or a Table (a group). For a table, set the seats per table from 2 to 50, the price per table, how many tables are available and the maximum tables per order.
  • Guests pick a type, then a quantity. On the event page, guests use plus and minus buttons for each ticket type, up to 20 per order. Nothing is pre-selected, so the guest always chooses.
  • A table is the minimum. A table is bought by the table. A table of 8 issues 8 tickets, even if fewer people come.
  • Send each seat to a friend. At checkout, each extra ticket can have a guest name and email, and that guest gets their own QR ticket by email. A ticket left blank stays with the buyer, who can send it later from the order page.
  • Know what sending does. Sending a ticket gives it a new code, so the buyer's old copy stops working. The buyer gets one receipt with a link to every ticket.
  • Refund or cancel a whole order. In the admin, open the event and choose Orders. You can refund or cancel an entire order, which cancels the unused tickets.

Group orders work on free and paid events. Paid tickets go through your own Stripe account, so the money goes straight to you, with no per-ticket fees from DoorPass.

Questions venues ask

Can one person buy tickets for a whole group?

Yes. A buyer can order up to 20 tickets in one order and send each one to a friend by name and email.

How do tables work?

A table is bought as one unit with a set number of seats, from 2 to 50. Buying a table of 8 issues 8 tickets.

What happens when a buyer sends a ticket to a friend?

The friend gets their own QR ticket by email, and the buyer's old copy of that ticket stops working.

Can we sell paid tickets with DoorPass?

DoorPass runs free and paid events and door lists. Paid tickets go through your own Stripe account, so the money goes straight to you, with no per-ticket fees from DoorPass.
