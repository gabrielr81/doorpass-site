DoorPass / Tours, classes and timed entry

Ticketing for tours, classes and timed entry

Tee times, tours and classes all run on time slots. DoorPass builds a day of slots in one step, caps each one, and checks guests in at the right time.

Request access See the live demo

What a tour, class or timed-entry venue gets

  • Generate a whole day. Enter the first start, the last start, every N minutes, and the spots in each slot. DoorPass creates the slots.
  • Or add them one by one. Add a slot with a start, an optional end, its spots and an optional name, such as a guide or a class level.
  • Guests pick a slot. The slot is kept on the order and shows on the order page, the ticket page, the calendar file and the guest list CSV.
  • The scanner checks the time. A slot ticket is valid from 30 minutes before the slot starts until it ends. Outside that: "Wrong time slot. Ticket NOT used." This works offline too.
  • Door sales pick a slot. Walk-ups are recorded as cash, card reader or comp against a slot, so every slot count stays right.
  • Groups and extras. Sell group tickets, and add priced extras such as parking or merch to the same order.
  • Email everyone with a ticket. Message everyone with a ticket, only those not checked in yet, or only those already in.

Events with no time slots work as before. A waitlist is not available on events with time slots.

Questions venues ask

Can I create a full day of time slots at once?

Yes. Enter the first start, the last start, the gap in minutes and the spots per slot, and DoorPass generates them.

What happens if a guest arrives at the wrong time?

The scanner shows "Wrong time slot. Ticket NOT used." A slot ticket is valid from 30 minutes before the slot starts until it ends. A desk admin can still admit the guest by hand.

Do guests need an app?

No. Guests get a QR ticket by email and staff scan in a browser.

What happens if the Wi-Fi drops at the door?

Open the scanner on Wi-Fi before doors. It keeps scanning offline and syncs when the connection is back.

Can we sell paid tickets with DoorPass?

You can run free events, door lists and test-mode checkout today. Card payments with Stripe are coming soon.
