DoorPass / Fundraiser and golf tournament ticketing

Ticketing for fundraisers, galas and golf tournaments

Galas sell tables, tournaments sell tee times, and both sell extras. DoorPass has presets for each, so the setup starts with the right ticket types.

Request access See the live demo

What a fundraiser or tournament gets

  • Fundraiser and golf presets. Admin, New event offers Fundraiser/gala and Golf/sports tournament presets with wording, ticket types (including tables), entry rule and starter text. Everything stays editable, and the event starts hidden.
  • Tables for sponsors and guests. Make a table ticket with 2 to 50 seats and a price per table. The buyer can send each seat to a friend, and every seat is a QR ticket.
  • Tee times as time slots. Generate a day of slots, every N minutes, with spots in each. A slot ticket is valid from 30 minutes before the slot starts until it ends.
  • Priced extras. Add extras such as parking, merch or drink packages to the same order.
  • Promo and comp codes. Create percent-off, dollars-off or free codes for sponsors and volunteers, limited to a ticket type, a number of uses or a last day.
  • Name badges. Print a name badge when a guest is admitted, or print the whole list, on a DYMO LabelWriter with 30857 labels.
  • Check-in in the browser. Staff scan with a phone camera. Open the scanner on Wi-Fi first and it keeps scanning offline.

DoorPass is for tickets, tables, check-in and reports. It is not a donor or giving platform. Card payments with Stripe are coming soon.

Questions venues ask

Can we sell tables for a gala?

Yes. A table ticket has 2 to 50 seats and one price per table. Buying a table issues one QR ticket per seat.

Can DoorPass handle tee times?

Yes. Add time slots, one by one or generated across the day, each with its own spots. The scanner checks that a guest arrives within the slot.

Can sponsors and volunteers get free or discounted tickets?

Yes. Create a free, percent-off or dollars-off code and limit it to a ticket type, a number of uses or a last day.

What happens if the Wi-Fi drops at the door?

Open the scanner on Wi-Fi before doors. It keeps scanning offline and syncs when the connection is back.

Can we sell paid tickets with DoorPass?

You can run free events, door lists and test-mode checkout today. Card payments with Stripe are coming soon.
