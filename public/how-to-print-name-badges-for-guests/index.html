How to print name badges for guests

For member nights, conferences and private events, a badge with the guest's name helps staff and guests alike. DoorPass prints one at check-in, or prints the whole list ahead of time.

Request access See the live demo

The steps

  • Use the common badge setup. DoorPass prints for a DYMO LabelWriter 550, 5XL or 450 with DYMO 30857 name badge labels, which are 4 by 2.25 inches.
  • Print a badge as guests arrive. After a guest is admitted in the scanner, tap Print name badge. The same button is on the ticket's staff panel, and at the front desk.
  • Or print them all ahead of time. In the admin, open the event and choose Name badges. Print all guests, or only those who have checked in. The badges are sorted by last name, and only admins can print them.
  • Know what is on the badge. A centered logo with the event title, the guest's first name in large type, the last name and ticket type, and a QR code with a backup code. Long names shrink to fit.
  • Scan the badge at the bar. The QR code on the badge works at perk stations, so a guest can redeem a perk without finding their phone.

Badges print in black and white. They keep DoorPass branding even if your venue uses its own logo in the header.

Questions venues ask

Which printer and labels does DoorPass support for name badges?

A DYMO LabelWriter 550, 5XL or 450 with DYMO 30857 name badge labels, which are 4 by 2.25 inches.

Can we print all the badges before the event?

Yes. In the admin, open the event and choose Name badges to print all guests or only checked-in guests, sorted by last name.

Who can print the whole list of badges?

Admins. Staff can print a single badge from the scanner after a guest is admitted.

Do guests or staff need to download an app?

No. Guests get their QR ticket by email. Staff open DoorPass in a browser on a phone or laptop and use the camera to scan.
