DoorPass / Gym and studio ticketing

Ticketing for gyms, studios and classes

Drop-in classes, workshops and open houses all need a simple booking and a front desk that knows who is walking in. DoorPass covers both in the browser.

What a gym, studio or class business gets

  • Start from a Class preset. Admin, New event offers a Class/studio/gym preset with Drop-in and Member ticket types, a Book your spot page and an experience-level question. Everything stays editable, and the event starts hidden.
  • Class times as time slots. Add slots one by one, or generate a day every N minutes, each with its own spots. A slot ticket is valid from 30 minutes before the slot starts until it ends.
  • Reusable passes. A reusable pass can be scanned every time a member comes in, as often as they like, and every visit is logged.
  • Front desk mode with photos. Scanning shows the member's photo, member-since date and visit count. Take the photo from the scan result or the profile, and ask first. Photos are visible to staff only.
  • Questions for guests. Ask experience level, injuries to know about or shirt size. Answers show on the ticket, at the scanner and in the guest list download.
  • Email everyone who booked. Message everyone with a ticket, only those not checked in yet, or only those already in.
  • Staff roles. Front desk only, door only and perks only roles keep each person to the job they do.

A reusable pass is a ticket that can be scanned many times. DoorPass does not bill monthly memberships, renew them or freeze them.

Questions venues ask

Can DoorPass bill monthly gym memberships?

No. DoorPass sells and checks in tickets and reusable passes. It does not bill monthly dues, renew memberships or freeze them.

Can the front desk see a member's photo when they scan?

Yes. Front desk mode shows the photo, member-since date and visit count at the scan. Ask for permission before taking a photo. Photos are visible to staff only.

Can we run classes at set times with limited spots?

Yes. Add time slots, one by one or generated across the day, each with its own number of spots.

What happens if the Wi-Fi drops at the door?

Open the scanner on Wi-Fi before doors. It keeps scanning offline and syncs when the connection is back.

Can we sell paid tickets with DoorPass?

DoorPass runs free and paid events and door lists. Paid tickets go through your own Stripe account, so the money goes straight to you, with no per-ticket fees from DoorPass.
