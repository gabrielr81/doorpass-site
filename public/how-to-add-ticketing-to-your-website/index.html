How to add ticketing to your own website

You do not have to send guests away from your site. Add two lines of code and your upcoming events appear on your page, with checkout in a pop-up that matches your look.

Get started See the live demo

The steps

  • Allow your website. In DoorPass, open Settings and choose Website embed. Add your website's address and save. No redeploy is needed.
  • Paste two lines where events should appear. Settings shows the two lines to paste: a script tag, and an events tag. The list uses your website's own fonts, text color and link color.
  • Guests pick a ticket, then a number. Get tickets opens checkout as a pop-up in the same look. Nothing is pre-selected, so the guest chooses the ticket type or table and then the quantity.
  • Use your own buttons if you prefer. Add a button for each event that names the event, and the same checkout opens.
  • Match your brand. Set the accent color, background, text and fonts, and add a function that runs when an order completes.
  • Check your site's security policy. If your site sets a Content-Security-Policy, allow your DoorPass address for scripts, connections, frames and images.

Orders, emails and door check-in all stay in DoorPass. If DoorPass can't be reached or the website isn't on the list, the widget shows a link to your DoorPass events instead. Events also carry Google event data, so they can appear in Google's event results.

Questions venues ask

Do I need a developer to add ticketing to my website?

No for the events list: paste the two lines shown in Settings under Website embed. Custom buttons and branding take a little more code.

Do guests leave my website to buy tickets?

No. Checkout opens as a pop-up on your page in your site's look.

Where do orders and the guest list go?

Into your DoorPass. Orders, emails and door check-in all stay there.

Can we sell paid tickets with DoorPass?

DoorPass runs free and paid events and door lists. Paid tickets go through your own Stripe account, so the money goes straight to you, with no per-ticket fees from DoorPass.
