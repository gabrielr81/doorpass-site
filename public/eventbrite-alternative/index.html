What a small venue gets with DoorPass

  • QR tickets by email. Guests can add the event to their calendar. No app for them to download.
  • Scan with the phones you already have. Staff open DoorPass in a browser and point the camera at the ticket.
  • Offline door mode. Open the scanner on Wi-Fi before doors; it keeps scanning if the connection drops and syncs when it returns.
  • Group orders, perks and member photo check-in. One buyer, many tickets; free drinks and VIP lines redeemed at the door; a member's photo shows at the scan.
  • Your data stays yours. Your own isolated copy. Export your guest list any time, or connect your own CRM.

When Eventbrite is the better fit

If you need paid card checkout today, run conferences or classes, or count on Eventbrite's marketplace to find new buyers, Eventbrite is a solid choice. DoorPass is for venues and promoters who run the door every week and want their own system: their own ticket emails, their own guest list, and staff scanning on the phones they already have.

Questions venues ask

Is DoorPass an Eventbrite alternative?

Yes, for venues and promoters who run events every week. DoorPass gives your venue its own ticketing and door system: QR tickets by email, phone-camera scanning in the browser, offline mode, and your own copy of your guest list.

How are DoorPass fees different from Eventbrite fees?

Eventbrite charges per paid ticket. In the US that is a 3.7% + $1.79 service fee per ticket plus 2.9% payment processing per order, paid by buyers by default (as of November 2026). DoorPass is a monthly plan for your venue.

Can we sell paid tickets with DoorPass?

You can run free events, door lists and test-mode checkout today. Card payments with Stripe are coming soon.

Do guests need an app?

No. Guests get a QR ticket by email. Door staff scan it with any phone or laptop camera in the browser.

Can we move our events from Eventbrite?

Yes. Events start from presets, so the first one takes minutes. Send us your upcoming dates and we help you set them up.
