How to redeem drink perks at the bar

A ticket can include a perk, such as a drink token. Bar staff scan the guest's QR code and tap the perk, and DoorPass makes sure each one is used only once.

Request access See the live demo

The steps

  • Attach perks to a ticket type. Perks are set up with the ticket type, for example a drink token with a quantity. Confirm them when you set up the event.
  • Give bar staff a perks-only login. In the admin, open Staff and choose Invite by email. The Station role sees the perks screen only. Door leads and admins can redeem perks too.
  • Scan the guest's QR code. On the Perks screen, point the camera at the guest's QR code. The screen shows the guest and the perks they have.
  • Tap the perk to redeem it. Tap the perk. Each perk works once, so a second attempt is refused.
  • Stay online at the bar. Perk redemption needs a connection so a drink can't be redeemed twice, even if the door was running offline.
  • Review it afterward. The admin Log records what happened during the night.

Perks are separate from the door. A guest can be checked in at the door and redeem perks later at the bar, coat check or any other station.

Questions venues ask

What is a perk?

A benefit attached to a ticket type, such as a drink token. Staff redeem it with a QR scan.

Can a guest use a perk twice?

No. Each perk works once. Redemption needs a connection so it can't be used twice.

Who can redeem perks?

Station staff, door leads and admins. Door staff and front desk staff cannot.

Do guests or staff need to download an app?

No. Guests get their QR ticket by email. Staff open DoorPass in a browser on a phone or laptop and use the camera to scan.
