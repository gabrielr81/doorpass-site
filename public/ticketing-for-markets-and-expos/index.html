DoorPass / Market and expo ticketing

Ticketing for markets, expos and pop-ups

A market has visitors and vendors, often over several days. DoorPass gives each its own ticket and checks them in at the gate in the browser.

Request access See the live demo

What a market or expo gets

  • Start from a Market preset. Admin, New event offers a Market/expo preset with Visitor and Vendor ticket types, an hours and location page and a question about what vendors sell. Everything stays editable, and the event starts hidden.
  • Day passes for multi-day shows. On an event that spans several days, tick which days each ticket type is valid on. The scanner admits a day pass once per valid day and refuses it on a wrong day with Not valid today. Ticket NOT used.
  • Free or priced visitor tickets. Run free entry, or set prices and promo codes for early birds and sponsors. Card payments with Stripe are coming soon.
  • Extras. Add priced extras such as parking or merch to the same order.
  • Re-entry at the gate. The preset allows re-entry, so visitors can leave and come back.
  • Vendor check-in. Give vendors their own ticket type so you can see who has arrived and email the ones who have not.
  • Offline scanning. Open the scanner on Wi-Fi first. It keeps scanning offline and syncs when the connection is back.

DoorPass does not take vendor booth payments or assign booth locations. Vendors are a ticket type for check-in and communication.

Questions venues ask

Can we sell day passes for a two-day market?

Yes. Mark which days each ticket type is valid on. A day pass works once on each valid day and is refused on other days.

Can vendors check in separately from visitors?

Yes. The Market preset has Visitor and Vendor ticket types, so each group has its own ticket and its own count at the gate.

Does DoorPass handle vendor booth fees or maps?

No. It handles tickets, check-in and email. It does not collect booth fees or assign booth spots.

What happens if the Wi-Fi drops at the door?

Open the scanner on Wi-Fi before doors. It keeps scanning offline and syncs when the connection is back.

Can we sell paid tickets with DoorPass?

You can run free events, door lists and test-mode checkout today. Card payments with Stripe are coming soon.
