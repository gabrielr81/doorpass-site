DoorPass / Member club ticketing

Ticketing and check-in for member clubs

At a member club the front desk should know who is walking in. DoorPass checks members in with a QR scan and shows their photo and history on the same screen.

Request access See the live demo

What a member club gets

  • A front desk mode. Each scan checks the member in and shows their photo, member-since date, visit count and recent check-ins.
  • Photos you control. Take a photo from the scan result or the person's profile with a webcam, a phone camera or an upload. Ask the member first. Photos are visible to staff only.
  • Events for members and guests. Run member nights, classes and guest events with free or priced tickets, a guest list import and a waitlist when you sell out.
  • Name badges. Print a name badge when a guest is admitted, or print the whole list, on a DYMO LabelWriter with 30857 labels.
  • Email your members. Send a message to everyone with a ticket, only those not checked in yet, or only those already in.
  • Member data stays yours. Guests can delete their own data, and a member's photo goes with it. Each club runs its own copy of DoorPass.

DoorPass is for tickets, guest lists and check-in. It does not bill membership dues.

Questions venues ask

Can the front desk see a member's photo at check-in?

Yes. In Front desk mode each scan shows the member's photo, member-since date, visit count and recent check-ins.

Who can see member photos?

Staff only. Photos are deleted along with the guest's data.

Does DoorPass bill membership dues?

No. DoorPass is for tickets, guest lists and check-in, including member check-in at the front desk.

Do guests or staff need to download an app?

No. Guests get their QR ticket by email. Staff open DoorPass in a browser on a phone or laptop and use the camera to scan.
