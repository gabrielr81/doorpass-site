Where DoorPass is different

  • No app for anyone. Guests get a QR ticket by email. Staff scan in the browser on a phone or laptop.
  • Offline door mode. Open the scanner on Wi-Fi before doors; it keeps scanning if the connection drops and syncs when it returns.
  • Your own copy. Your venue's guest list is isolated and exportable. We don't sell or share it.
  • Built for the door. Group orders, perks redeemed at the bar, and member photo check-in.

When another platform is the better fit

If you need paid ticket sales today, or you want a marketplace or fan app that brings buyers to your events, one of the platforms above may suit you better, and each page says when. DoorPass is for the independent room that wants to run its own list and its own door.

Questions venues ask

What is the best ticketing platform for a small venue?

It depends on your room. Ticket Tailor, TicketLeap and SimpleTix publish low per-ticket fees. DICE, Posh and Shotgun bring their own fan audiences. DoorPass is for clubs and venues up to 500 capacity that want their own guest list and a door that scans in the browser with no app.

Which of these platforms publish their fees?

Universe, Eventbrite, Ticket Tailor, TicketLeap, SimpleTix and Luma publish per-ticket fees. DICE, Tixr, Etix, AXS and ShowClix do not publish a rate that we could find, so ask for the all-in fee at your ticket price.

Do guests or staff need to download an app?

No. Guests get their QR ticket by email. Staff open DoorPass in a browser on a phone or laptop and use the camera to scan.

Can we sell paid tickets with DoorPass?

DoorPass runs free and paid events and door lists. Paid tickets go through your own Stripe account, so the money goes straight to you, with no per-ticket fees from DoorPass.

Do we keep our guest data?

Yes. Each venue has its own isolated copy. We don't sell or share it, you can export it any time, and we delete it when you leave.
