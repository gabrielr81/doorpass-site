Terms of service

These terms describe how DoorPass works for venues. DoorPass is made by Revenue Copilot (the "provider"). Questions: gabriel@getrevenuecopilot.com.

What DoorPass is

DoorPass is web software for event tickets and door check-in. Each venue gets its own isolated copy, either hosted by the provider or on a dedicated server.

Your account and your venue data

  • You own your venue data: guests, tickets, orders, check-ins and photos you collect.
  • You are responsible for having the right to collect that data from your guests, including photo consent for member check-in.
  • The provider does not sell, share or use your venue data, except as needed to run and support the service.

Backups and deletion

Your copy keeps the latest one or two backups. You can download the latest backup from Settings and choose an email address to receive it. When you leave, the provider deletes your copy and its backups on request.

Payments

Card payments through Stripe are not available yet. DoorPass runs free and paid events, with checkout through the venue's own Stripe account. Terms for payments, fees and refunds will be added before payments go live.

Pricing and billing

Launch pricing is shown on the pricing section of the home page and may change before general sale. The final billing terms will be added here.

Availability

The provider works to keep DoorPass available during your events but does not guarantee uninterrupted service. Offline scanning is built to help at the door; keep a backup connection and a printed guest list for critical events.
