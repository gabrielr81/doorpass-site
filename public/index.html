Two ways to run DoorPass

Either way you get your own isolated copy. The difference is who runs the server.

Hosted

For venues under 1,000 capacity running one event at a time.

  • We run the servers. You sign up and get your own address, like yourvenue.doorpass.vip.
  • Your own copy of the app and your own database, separate from every other venue.
  • Backups inside your copy, with a download button and an optional email to you.
  • Live within a day of signing up.

Dedicated

For large venues, several events at the same time, or strict IT rules.

  • A server set up just for you, on your domain.
  • Room for multiple simultaneous events and larger crowds.
  • Your own CRM connected from day one, if you want it.
  • Set up with you, with a call before the first event.

Your data stays yours

Your data stays yours: your own isolated copy, we don't sell, share or use it, you can export or send it to your CRM any time, and we delete it when you leave.

  • Export your guests, tickets and check-ins whenever you like.

  • Ask us to delete everything when you leave.

Questions venues ask

What is DoorPass?

DoorPass is an event ticketing and door check-in system for venues, nightclubs, member clubs and event promoters. Guests get QR tickets by email, and door staff scan them with any phone or laptop camera in the browser, with no app to install. It keeps scanning offline, and each venue gets its own copy of its guest data.

How is DoorPass different from Ticketmaster or Eventbrite?

Ticketmaster and Eventbrite are ticket marketplaces: guests buy through the platform's own site and account, often with service fees added at checkout. DoorPass is a ticketing and door system you run as your own venue, on a monthly plan. Guests get tickets from you, your staff scan them with phones you already have, and your guest list stays in your own copy that you can export any time.

What equipment do we need at the door?

A phone or laptop with a camera and a browser for each scanner. A travel router or phone hotspot as a backup connection is a good idea, but offline mode covers short drops.

What happens if the internet goes down during doors?

Open the scanner on Wi-Fi before doors. It then keeps scanning offline and syncs when the connection is back.

Who owns our guest data?

You do. Each venue has its own isolated copy. We don't sell, share or use it, you can export it any time, and we delete it when you leave.

Can we use our own CRM?

Yes. DoorPass works fine without one. When you're ready, connect your own Twenty CRM from Settings, or ask us to set it up for you.

How long until we're live?

Hosted venues are usually ready within a day of signing up. Dedicated servers take a few days because we set them up with you.

Can we sell tickets online today?

You can run free events, door lists and test-mode checkout today. Card payments with Stripe are coming soon.
