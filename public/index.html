Questions venues ask

What is DoorPass?

DoorPass is an event ticketing and door check-in system for venues, nightclubs, member clubs, promoters and local sports leagues. Guests get QR tickets by email, and door staff scan them with any phone or laptop camera in the browser, with no app to install. It keeps scanning offline, and each venue gets its own copy of its guest data.

How is DoorPass different from Ticketmaster or Eventbrite?

Ticketmaster and Eventbrite are ticket marketplaces: guests buy through the platform's own site and account, often with service fees added at checkout. DoorPass is a ticketing and door system you run as your own venue, on one flat monthly plan with no per-ticket fees. Guests buy from you, your staff scan them with phones you already have, and your guest list stays in your own copy that you can export any time.

Does DoorPass take a cut of ticket sales?

No. DoorPass charges one flat monthly price and never touches your money. Paid tickets go through your own Stripe account, so sales land directly with you. Stripe's own card processing fees still apply.

What equipment do we need at the door?

A phone or laptop with a camera and a browser for each scanner. A travel router or phone hotspot as a backup connection is a good idea, but offline mode covers short drops.

What happens if the internet goes down during doors?

Open the scanner on Wi-Fi before doors. It then keeps scanning offline and syncs when the connection is back.

Who owns our guest data?

You do. Each venue has its own isolated copy. We don't sell or share it, you can export it any time, and we delete it when you leave.

Can we use our own CRM?

Yes. DoorPass works fine without one. Hosted venues can connect their own Twenty CRM for free with an API key from Settings. Dedicated venues can have us set it up for a one-time $199.

What are the Hosted plan limits?

Hosted is for venues up to 500 capacity running up to 10 events a month. Bigger venues, or venues running more events or several at once, move to Dedicated with no limits.

What happens after we sign up?

Hosted venues sign up online and start setting up right away. For Dedicated, we call you within the next business day to plan your server and launch together.
