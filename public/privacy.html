Privacy notice

Your data stays yours: your own isolated copy, we don't sell, share or use it, you can export or send it to your CRM any time, and we delete it when you leave.

This website

When you send the request-access form, we receive the details you type (name, venue, role, capacity, events per month, current ticketing, email and phone). We use them to reply and set up DoorPass for you. This website does not use advertising trackers.

Venue copies of DoorPass

  • Each venue has its own isolated copy and its own database.
  • Venues decide what guest data they collect. Venues are responsible for notices and consent to their guests.
  • Venues can export their data, download their latest backup and ask for deletion.

Backups

Each copy keeps the latest one or two backups inside the venue's own copy. A venue can add an email address to receive its backup.
