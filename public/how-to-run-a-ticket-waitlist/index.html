How to run a waitlist when your event sells out

Sold out is not the end. DoorPass lets guests join a waitlist and offers seats to them in order as soon as any come free.

Request access See the live demo

The steps

  • Guests join when it sells out. When an event or a ticket type sells out, guests can join the waitlist on the event page.
  • Freed seats are offered automatically. Seats that free up from a refund, a cancelled order, a checkout that timed out or a higher capacity are offered in the order people joined.
  • The guest gets 2 hours. The guest gets an email with a link, and the seats are held for them for 2 hours.
  • Watch the list. In the admin, open the event and choose Waitlist. It shows who is waiting, who has an offer and who claimed.
  • Step in when you want to. Offer now sends seats to someone early, if enough are free. Remove takes someone off the list.
  • If email is not set up. The page shows the offer link, so you can send it yourself.

Raising the capacity is the quickest way to release seats to the waitlist.

Questions venues ask

How does the waitlist decide who gets a seat?

Freed seats are offered in the order people joined the waitlist.

How long does a waitlisted guest have to claim a seat?

The seats are held for 2 hours from the email offer.

Can I offer seats to someone early?

Yes. In the admin, open the event, choose Waitlist, and use Offer now, as long as enough seats are free.

Do guests or staff need to download an app?

No. Guests get their QR ticket by email. Staff open DoorPass in a browser on a phone or laptop and use the camera to scan.
