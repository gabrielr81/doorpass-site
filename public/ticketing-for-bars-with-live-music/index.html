What a bar with a stage gets

  • Free or cover-charge tickets. Sell tickets for a show or list them free. Each guest gets a QR code by email and a calendar file. DoorPass runs free and paid events and door lists. Paid tickets go through your own Stripe account, so the money goes straight to you, with no per-ticket fees from DoorPass.
  • Door sales. Record walk-ups as cash, card reader or comp, so your counts and capacity stay right.
  • Drink tokens and extras. Attach drink tokens as perks, and sell priced extras such as parking, merch or drink packages in the same order.
  • Sold out? Use the waitlist. Guests can join a waitlist, and freed seats are offered in order and held for 2 hours.
  • Put shows on your website. Add two lines of code and your upcoming shows appear on your site, with checkout in a pop-up. Event pages also carry Google event data.
  • A door any bartender can run. Staff scan with a phone camera in the browser. Open it on Wi-Fi first and it keeps scanning offline.

Each venue runs its own copy of DoorPass with its own guest data.

Questions venues ask

Can a bar sell tickets for live music?

Yes. Sell free or priced tickets, record door sales, and scan guests in with a phone camera.

Can we sell extras with a ticket?

Yes. Priced extras such as parking, merch or drink packages can be added to the same order.

What if the bar is full?

Set a capacity. When a show sells out, guests can join a waitlist and are offered freed seats in order.

What happens if the Wi-Fi drops at the door?

Open the scanner on Wi-Fi before doors. It keeps scanning offline and syncs when the connection is back.

Can we sell paid tickets with DoorPass?

DoorPass runs free and paid events and door lists. Paid tickets go through your own Stripe account, so the money goes straight to you, with no per-ticket fees from DoorPass.
