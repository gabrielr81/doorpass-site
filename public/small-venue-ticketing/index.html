Questions venues ask

What should a small venue look for in a ticketing system?

QR tickets guests get by email, door scanning on the phones you already have, a door that keeps working when the Wi-Fi drops, staff roles, and a guest list you own and can export. DoorPass is built around exactly those.

How many people can DoorPass check in?

The hosted plan is built for venues under 1,000 capacity, with as many phone or laptop scanners as you need at the door. Bigger venues get their own dedicated server.

Do we need special scanners or hardware?

No. Any modern phone or laptop with a camera and a browser works as a scanner.

Can we sell paid tickets?

You can run free events, door lists and test-mode checkout today. Card payments with Stripe are coming soon.
