DoorPass / Church and community ticketing

Ticketing for churches and community events

A community event needs a simple sign-up, a friendly check-in and a way to reach everyone who is coming. DoorPass covers all three in the browser.

Request access See the live demo

What a church or community group gets

  • Start from a Church or community preset. Admin, New event offers a Church/community preset that fills in the wording, ticket types and starter page text. Everything stays editable, and the event starts hidden so nothing goes live by accident.
  • Free events and guest lists. Run free events, or paste a guest list of up to 300 names with emails. Every guest gets a QR code by email.
  • A check-in desk. Front desk mode checks people in with a QR scan and shows their photo, member-since date and visit count.
  • Photos with permission. Take a photo from the scan result or the profile. Ask first. Photos are visible to staff only and go when the guest's data is deleted.
  • Volunteers get the right access. Roles include door only, front desk only and perks only, so a volunteer sees only what the job needs.
  • Email everyone who is coming. Message everyone with a ticket, only those not checked in yet, or only those already in.
  • When it fills up. Set a capacity. When an event sells out, guests can join a waitlist and are offered freed seats in order.

DoorPass is for tickets, guest lists and check-in. It is not a donor or giving platform, and it does not bill membership dues.

Questions venues ask

Can a church run free events with a sign-up list?

Yes. Free tickets and guest list imports both give every guest a QR code by email.

Can volunteers check people in on their own phones?

Yes. Volunteers open DoorPass in a browser and scan with the phone camera. No app is needed, and roles limit what each volunteer can see.

Does DoorPass take donations?

No. DoorPass is for tickets, guest lists and check-in. It is not a donor or giving platform.

Do we keep our guest data?

Yes. Each venue has its own isolated copy. We don't sell, share or use it, you can export it any time, and we delete it when you leave.

Do guests or staff need to download an app?

No. Guests get their QR ticket by email. Staff open DoorPass in a browser on a phone or laptop and use the camera to scan.
