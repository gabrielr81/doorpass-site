DoorPass / Comedy club and theater ticketing

Ticketing for comedy clubs and small theaters

A comedy club sells the same room several times a night. DoorPass lets one event carry every showtime, with its own spots, and checks guests in at the right one.

What a comedy club or small theater gets

  • Showtimes in one event. In the event editor, add time slots one by one (start, optional end, spots, optional name) or generate a whole night at once, such as every 90 minutes.
  • Spots per showtime. Each slot has its own number of spots, so the early show and the late show sell separately.
  • The right show at the door. A slot ticket is valid from 30 minutes before the slot starts until it ends. Outside that window the scanner says "Wrong time slot. Ticket NOT used.", online or offline.
  • Tables sold by the table. Make a table ticket with 2 to 50 seats and a price per table. The buyer can send each seat to a friend, and every seat is a QR ticket.
  • Door sales pick a slot. Admins and door leads record walk-ups as cash, card reader or comp, and pick the showtime.
  • Calendar files and event data. Guests get the slot on their ticket page and in a calendar file, in their own time. Event pages carry Google event data.
  • A door any host can run. Staff scan with a phone or laptop camera in the browser. Open the scanner on Wi-Fi before doors and it keeps scanning offline.

DoorPass sells general admission and tables. It does not have a seat map or assigned seats, and a waitlist is not available on events with time slots.

Questions venues ask

Can one event have several showtimes?

Yes. Add time slots to the event, one by one or generated across the night, each with its own number of spots.

Can a guest use an early-show ticket at the late show?

No. A slot ticket works from 30 minutes before its slot starts until it ends. Outside that, the scanner refuses it and does not use the ticket. A desk admin can still admit someone by hand.

Does DoorPass have assigned seating?

No. DoorPass sells general admission and tables with 2 to 50 seats. It does not have a seat map.

What happens if the Wi-Fi drops at the door?

Open the scanner on Wi-Fi before doors. It keeps scanning offline and syncs when the connection is back.

Can we sell paid tickets with DoorPass?

You can run free events, door lists and test-mode checkout today. Card payments with Stripe are coming soon.
