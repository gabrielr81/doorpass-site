DoorPass / Conference and workshop ticketing

Registration and check-in for conferences and workshops

A conference needs registration with a few questions and a fast check-in line. DoorPass collects the answers and checks attendees in with a phone camera.

Request access See the live demo

What a conference or workshop gets

  • Start from a Conference preset. Admin, New event offers a Conference/workshop preset with Attendee, Student and Speaker ticket types, Register wording and questions for company, job title and dietary needs. Everything stays editable, and the event starts hidden.
  • Questions for every attendee. Answers show on the ticket, at the scanner and as columns in the guest list download.
  • Multi-day passes. Tick which days each ticket type is valid on. The scanner admits a day pass once per valid day.
  • Name badges. Print a name badge when a guest is admitted, or print the whole list, on a DYMO LabelWriter with 30857 labels.
  • Sessions as time slots. Use time slots for workshop sessions with their own spots. A slot ticket is valid from 30 minutes before the slot starts until it ends.
  • Email attendees. Message everyone with a ticket, only those not checked in yet, or only those already in.
  • Registration only, no door. The Online event preset is registration only, for webinars where the link is sent after registering.

DoorPass is for registration, tickets and check-in. It is not an agenda app, a speaker portal or a video platform.

Questions venues ask

Can we ask attendees for company and dietary needs?

Yes. The Conference preset includes company, job title and dietary needs questions, and you can add others. Answers appear in the guest list download.

Can we print name badges at check-in?

Yes. Badges print on a DYMO LabelWriter with 30857 labels, one per admitted guest or for the whole list.

Can we run workshops with limited seats at set times?

Yes. Add time slots with their own number of spots. Workshop sessions can be separate slots.

What happens if the Wi-Fi drops at the door?

Open the scanner on Wi-Fi before doors. It keeps scanning offline and syncs when the connection is back.

Can we sell paid tickets with DoorPass?

You can run free events, door lists and test-mode checkout today. Card payments with Stripe are coming soon.
