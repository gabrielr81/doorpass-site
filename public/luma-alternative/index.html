What a small venue gets with DoorPass

  • QR tickets by email. Guests can add the event to their calendar. No app for them to download.
  • Scan with the phones you already have. Staff open DoorPass in a browser and point the camera at the ticket.
  • Offline door mode. Open the scanner on Wi-Fi before doors; it keeps scanning if the connection drops and syncs when it returns.
  • Group orders, perks and member photo check-in. One buyer, many tickets; free drinks and VIP lines redeemed at the door; a member's photo shows at the scan.
  • Your data stays yours. Your own isolated copy. Export your guest list any time, or connect your own CRM.

When Luma is the better fit

If you run meetups, a recurring series or a community calendar, want guests to follow your events, and need paid tickets today, Luma is a strong choice. DoorPass is for the venue with a door line and a guest list to manage.

Questions venues ask

Is DoorPass a Luma alternative?

Yes, for clubs and venues that run a door. DoorPass is ticketing and door check-in for venues, clubs, member clubs and promoters up to 500 capacity. Luma is the better choice for community events and calendars, and for paid tickets today.

Do guests or staff need to download an app?

No. Guests get their QR ticket by email. Staff open DoorPass in a browser on a phone or laptop and use the camera to scan.

What happens if the Wi-Fi drops at the door?

Open the scanner on Wi-Fi before doors. It keeps scanning offline and syncs when the connection is back.

Can we sell paid tickets with DoorPass?

DoorPass runs free and paid events and door lists. Paid tickets go through your own Stripe account, so the money goes straight to you, with no per-ticket fees from DoorPass.

Do we keep our guest data?

Yes. Each venue has its own isolated copy. We don't sell or share it, you can export it any time, and we delete it when you leave.
