How to check in guests with QR codes

You don't need scanners or an app. Door staff open DoorPass in a browser on a phone or laptop, point the camera at the guest's QR code, and read the screen color.

Request access See the live demo

The steps

  • Before doors: sign in and open Scan. Sign in on Wi-Fi, open Scan, and wait for the Offline ready badge. Do this on every phone that will scan.
  • Scan the guest's QR code. Point the camera at the QR code on the guest's ticket. Tap Scan next after each guest.
  • Read the screen color. Green means let them in. Amber (Check ID) means the ticket was already used: read the message and ask for ID. Red means do not admit: send the guest to the door lead.
  • No QR code? Use the backup. Every ticket has a short backup code. Choose Type code and enter it, or choose Search name and find the guest.
  • Fix a mistake. Tap Undo within one minute if you admitted the wrong guest.
  • Re-entry events. When a guest leaves, tap Scan out. Scan again when they return.

Guests get their QR ticket by email, so they have nothing to install. Staff use the same login they use for the rest of DoorPass, and each role sees only what it needs.

Questions venues ask

Do guests or staff need to download an app?

No. Guests get their QR ticket by email. Staff open DoorPass in a browser on a phone or laptop and use the camera to scan.

What if a guest can't find their QR code?

Use the backup code printed on the ticket, or search for the guest by name.

What do the screen colors mean?

Green means let them in. Amber means the ticket was already used, so check ID. Red means do not admit.
