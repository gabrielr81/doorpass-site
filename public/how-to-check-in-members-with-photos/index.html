How to check in members with photos

For gyms, clubs and churches, the front desk should see who is standing there. DoorPass's Front desk mode checks the member in and shows their photo on the same scan.

Get started See the live demo

The steps

  • Give the front desk its own login. In the admin, open Staff and choose Invite by email. Pick the Front desk role, which opens the front desk only, or Door lead if the person also runs the door.
  • Open Front desk on the scanner. Sign in on a phone, tablet or laptop and choose Front desk from the menu.
  • Scan the member's QR code. Each scan checks the member in and shows their photo, member-since date, visit count and recent check-ins.
  • Take a photo if there isn't one. Choose Take photo from the scan result or the person's profile. A webcam, a phone camera or a file upload all work. Ask the member before taking one.
  • Keep photos private. Photos are visible to staff only. They are deleted along with a guest's data, and guests can delete their own data themselves.

The front desk runs in the browser, so there is nothing to install. On a phone, the camera steps aside after a scan so the member's photo and status fill the screen, and Scan next brings the camera back.

Questions venues ask

Can the front desk see a member's photo when they check in?

Yes. In Front desk mode each scan shows the member's photo, member-since date, visit count and recent check-ins.

Do we have to take a photo of every member?

No. Take a photo only after asking the member. You can use a webcam, a phone camera or a file upload.

Who can see member photos?

Staff only. Photos are deleted along with the guest's data.

Do guests or staff need to download an app?

No. Guests get their QR ticket by email. Staff open DoorPass in a browser on a phone or laptop and use the camera to scan.
