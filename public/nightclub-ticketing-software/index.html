What a nightclub gets

  • Guest lists and tickets together. Paste a guest list (first and last name, email, up to 300) or sell tickets. Every guest gets their own QR code by email.
  • Tables sold by the table. Make a table ticket with 2 to 50 seats and a price per table. The buyer can send each seat to a friend, and every seat is a QR ticket.
  • Credit your promoters. Add ?ref=name to an event link and tickets bought through it are credited to that name. The event report shows sales by promoter link.
  • A door that keeps scanning offline. Staff scan with any phone or laptop camera. Open the scanner on Wi-Fi before doors and it keeps working if the signal drops, then syncs.
  • Door sales and comps. Admins and door leads can record a door sale as cash, card reader or comp, so reports and capacity stay right.
  • Drink tokens at the bar. Attach perks such as a drink token to a ticket type. Bar staff scan and tap, and each perk works once.
  • Give each person the right access. Roles include door only, front desk only and perks only, so a bar back never sees the guest list.

Each venue runs its own copy of DoorPass with its own guest data. We don't sell or share it.

Questions venues ask

Can a nightclub run a guest list and sell tickets in DoorPass?

Yes. Paste a guest list of up to 300 names, or sell free and priced tickets. Every guest gets a QR code by email.

Can we sell tables?

Yes. A table ticket has 2 to 50 seats and one price per table. Buying a table issues one QR ticket per seat.

How do we credit promoters?

Add ?ref= and the promoter's name to the event link. Tickets bought through the link are credited to that name in the event report.

What happens if the Wi-Fi drops at the door?

Open the scanner on Wi-Fi before doors. It keeps scanning offline and syncs when the connection is back.

Can we sell paid tickets with DoorPass?

DoorPass runs free and paid events and door lists. Paid tickets go through your own Stripe account, so the money goes straight to you, with no per-ticket fees from DoorPass.
